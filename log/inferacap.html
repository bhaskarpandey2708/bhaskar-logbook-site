InferaCap

A cost-aware load balancer for AI inference that sends requests to idle, already-paid GPU capacity in other regions.

WhenOct 2026 – Present
WhereRemote
StatusPrototype, seeking pilots

The situation

Companies running AI models in several regions pay for GPUs that sit idle overnight in one region while another pays on-demand rates at its peak. Ordinary load balancers route by ping and know nothing about contracts or data-residency rules. In October 2026 I started InferaCap to route each request by cost, latency budget and residency law.

What I did

  • Built an OpenAI-compatible reverse proxy that pools vLLM, SGLang and Ollama clusters across regions.
  • Routed by contract order: owned hardware first, then committed reservations, then on-demand, within each request's latency budget.
  • Enforced data residency, so EU traffic stays in the EU, and added circuit breakers with automatic failover.
  • Built a free log scanner that estimates avoidable GPU spend from a company's existing logs, plus a capacity simulator and a web dashboard.
  • Ran a live worker on free Kaggle T4 GPUs, and wrote 65 automated tests.
  • Set up dual licensing: the scanner and simulator are open source, while the live gateway is commercial with a gain-share pilot.
