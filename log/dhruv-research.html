The situation

At Dhruv Research I moved from Area Manager (Strategy and Operations, Jan 2022) to Zonal Manager (Strategy and Operations Analytics, Apr 2023). In Sep 2023 I moved to the central team as Operations Analytics Lead, running operational analytics for the firm's pan-India projects, including its 2024 Lok Sabha election survey. The firm ran large market and social research projects, and my job was to make field operations measurable and faster.

What I did

  • From Sep 2023, led central operations analytics for pan-India projects, including the 2024 Lok Sabha election survey.
  • Built a metric-driven monitoring dashboard covering 260 team leaders on quality, operations and delivery.
  • Delivered BI reporting across 151 districts in 4 states, used by senior leadership.
  • Supported four long-term state projects and seven short-term projects.
  • Introduced a three-stage training programme and quality tracking on skip and probe behaviour.
  • Automated route planning and payroll and incentive calculation.
  • Did hands-on technical work too: multi-election data analysis, website DevOps, mobile app development and early AI models.
