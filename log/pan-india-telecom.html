Pan-India Telecom Intelligence

A telecom intelligence and geolocation engine covering all 22 licensed service areas of India, built on public numbering-plan data with no personal data stored.

WhenOct 2026 – Present
WhereIndia, remote
StatusPilot, release gates open

The situation

Field and outreach programmes need to know which circle, operator and area a telephone series belongs to, but India's numbering data is spread across Department of Telecommunications allocations, operator codes and switching areas. In October 2026 I built a single engine at Future-AI that resolves a number series down through operator, switching area, district and network layer, using only publicly allocated routing data.

What I did

  • Mapped all 22 licensed service areas across 28 states and 8 union territories: 1,883 DoT series and 18,830 five-digit gateway blocks.
  • Built the resolution chain: numbering series, gateway block, operator code, switching and charging area, district, and the cellular network layer.
  • Built a REST API with OpenAPI docs, batch checks, number-portability risk checks and an interactive map dashboard.
  • Added a quality layer: encrypted storage, benchmarks on PostgreSQL 16 and 18 in CI, and release gates that keep production blocked until every proof is supplied.
  • Kept it within the DPDP Act 2023: no personal data is stored or scraped; results map only to public routing series and network areas.
  • Wrote a regression suite of 347 unit and integration tests.
