The situation

In May 2024 I co-founded Arjuna Strategy and Consulting, a strategy and operations firm. It runs the full chain of public-facing programmes: public campaigns and social media, field operations and field studies, surveys, self-help group integration, women's cohort studies, election operations, call-centre and WhatsApp operations, and media and public relations. It also supports manufacturing businesses (see the MSME ZED stop). I am co-founder and technical lead.

What I did

  • Delivered programmes in Haryana, Jharkhand and Jammu and Kashmir, with smaller assignments in Assam and West Bengal.
  • Greater Bengaluru civic elections: Head of Operations for a 15-day assignment within a 400-ward survey that supported councillor candidate selection, leading the technical and operations teams together.
  • Tamil Nadu and Kerala: technical support for survey operations.
  • Ran field studies, self-help group integration and women's cohort studies alongside surveys.
  • Built a call-centre CRM used by 200+ callers and field agents, and ran call-centre and WhatsApp outreach.
  • Handled media and public relations work as part of the same operating model.
