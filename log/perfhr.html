PerfHR: performance and hiring systems that run every week

Co-founder. A firm that installs weekly performance and hiring systems: KPI architecture, role scorecards, weekly business reviews and structured talent acquisition.

WhenJan 2026 – Present
WhereIndia, remote
StatusActive

The situation

Most teams already have targets and reviews. What they lack is a rhythm that holds: clear measures, named owners and decisions that get closed. Without it, performance turns into follow-ups, noise and fatigue. Hiring breaks the same way, with slow closures and weak role fit. I co-founded PerfHR in January 2026 to treat performance and hiring as operating problems, at the point where HR, analytics and operations meet. The firm's clients include Sunlit Urbana (real estate), ClearDu (collections and service operations) and RSAP International (search and talent acquisition).

What I did

  • Built performance management systems for manufacturing companies, including makers of pumps and machinery.
  • Built performance metrics for large, people-heavy services organisations, a pharma company and real-estate firms.
  • Performance management was the first step toward lean management and Six Sigma: it prepared each organisation for them, and we then took clients through lean and Six Sigma work.
  • Ran senior hiring, including project heads and heads of accounts.
  • Our method: KPI architecture from outcomes to drivers to what a person can control, run through a weekly loop of signal, review, decision, owner, due date and closure.
  • Seven systems the firm installs: talent acquisition, a leadership dashboard, role scorecards (KRA and KPI), goal cycles, a weekly business review with a decision tracker, manager coaching, and PMS or HRMS rollout support.
  • AI stays human-in-the-loop: it captures signals and drafts the operating log, leaders approve decisions, and every action keeps an audit trail.
  • Built the company website myself: a Next.js front end with a Strapi CMS of 11 content types (pages, blog, resources, lead submissions and engagement events), a lead form, engagement tracking and SEO, deployed on Hostinger.
  • Published playbooks on why weekly performance reviews fail, role scorecards that managers use, and moving from KRAs to outcomes.

What I carried forward
