JS Strategy House: field research at constituency level

Ramnagar (UP) and Nanakmatta (Uttarakhand). Led three field teams and used data to choose 250+ booths and panchayats for outreach.

WhenMay 2021 – Jan 2022
WhereLucknow, with field work in Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand
StatusDelivered

The situation

At JS Strategy House I worked as a consultant on assembly constituency programmes in two states: Ramnagar in Uttar Pradesh and Nanakmatta in Uttarakhand. The job was to understand a place through data and then organise people on the ground.

What I did

  • Managed three teams of volunteers and organisers, including their training.
  • Analysed past patterns to pick 250+ booths and panchayats to prioritise.
  • Ran sentiment research to find which issues mattered where.

By the numbers

2constituencies in 2 states
3field teams led
250+booths and panchayats prioritised

What I carried forward

Data is only useful when it changes where people go tomorrow morning. That rule shaped the dashboards and route planners I built later.
