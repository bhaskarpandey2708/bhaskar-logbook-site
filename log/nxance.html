Nxance: investment analytics where every number is computed

Co-founder and CTO. Built the platform end to end: a statement reader tested on 1,000 statements, seven calculation engines, an in-house AI explainer and the security around them.

WhenApr 2026 – Present
WhereIndia, remote
StatusPre-launch

The situation

India has over 21 crore demat accounts and fewer than 1,000 SEBI-registered investment advisers. Most people hold mutual funds without knowing what they own, how much their funds overlap, or whether their goal is on track. Nxance, built by Ohshn Intelligence LLP with four founders, is a financial operating system with AI built in. It starts with portfolio analysis for mutual fund distributors, registered investment advisers and institutions, and will open to retail investors for self-analysis: set a goal, see what you own, reduce overlap, and see how the portfolio holds up when markets turn volatile. It is analytics and education: no personalised advice and no trades. As co-founder and CTO I designed and wrote the platform.

What I did

  • Statement reader: reads CAMS and KFintech consolidated account statements as PDFs (including password-protected ones), scanned PDFs, phone photos, CSV and Excel. Table rows are rebuilt from glyph positions with PDFium, and our own OCR pipeline on Tesseract handles tinted bands, near-blank pages and damaged fund codes.
  • Tested the reader on a 1,000-statement corpus with an answer key for each: on text PDFs 11,207 of 11,207 holdings and 314,854 of 314,854 cash flows read right. On photos and scans every holding is read right, left blank or blocked for the person to check. No silent errors.
  • Seven calculation engines: Health Check (allocation, HHI concentration, look-through exposure, true XIRR from statement cash flows, regular-vs-direct plan mix, goal maths and a 0-100 score with hard caps), Construction (a glide-path mix across equity, debt, gold and cash), Rebalance (5/25 drift bands), SIP redesign, a scenario sandbox, and behaviour and progress checks.
  • A ledger for every figure: each number is stored with its formula version and inputs. Anything that cannot be computed honestly is returned as not computed, with the reason, and the product says so.
  • Nxance AI explains reports without sending anything to an outside AI service: intent routing, retrieval of the person's own results, and Number Guard, which checks every number in an answer against the ledger. A 200-case evaluation set covers grounding, disclaimers, Hinglish and prompt injection.
  • Market data: a dated AMFI NAV store, holdings valued at the latest NAV, and fund quality ranked against each fund's own category.
  • Architecture: a Next.js gateway on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database (26 migrations) and a stateless Python FastAPI intelligence service reached by HMAC-signed requests. The service never learns who the user is.
  • Security and privacy: email-code, Google and Apple sign-in, a second factor for admins, field encryption at rest for holdings, reports and chat, statement files and passwords used once in memory and never stored, data export, account deletion with a 7-day cooling-off, audit logs and rate limits, all checked in a pre-launch security review.
  • Engineering discipline: CI with type checks, 52 Node test suites and 709 Python tests behind a 90% coverage gate, secret scanning, dependency and licence audits, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility checks, encrypted backups with restore drills, a deployment runbook and an investor demo mode.
  • Before this, a first phase (closed July 2026): a FastAPI prototype with a five-layer intelligence stack, models trained on synthetic India data, a Flutter Android app and a web portal.
